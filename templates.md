# Workflow templates

Ready-made n8n workflows for your Sharetribe marketplace. Pick one, click **Copy template JSON**, and paste it into n8n.

## Admin notifications

Get notified the moment something needs your attention.

- [Email on a new dispute](/templates/admin-notifications/admin-email-on-new-dispute)
- [Email on a new paid transaction](/templates/admin-notifications/admin-email-on-new-paid-transaction)

## Bookings

A smoother experience for buyers and providers around bookings.

- [Booking confirmation with calendar invite](/templates/bookings/booking-confirmation-with-calendar-invite)

## Monetization

Charge for listing or sign-up by gating Sharetribe approval behind a Stripe payment.

- [Approve listing after a Stripe Checkout fee](/templates/monetization/approve-listing-after-stripe-checkout-fee)
- [Approve user after a Stripe membership fee](/templates/monetization/approve-user-after-stripe-membership-fee)

## User onboarding

Connect new sign-ups to the tools where you run your marketing and ops.

- [Sync new users to Mailchimp](/templates/user-onboarding/sync-new-users-to-mailchimp)

Open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/tribebuilder/n8n-nodes-sharetribe/issues/new) and describe the job you want to automate. Contributions welcome - see [Contributing](/contributing).
