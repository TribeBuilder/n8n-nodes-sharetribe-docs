## What you can do with it

- Email your team the moment a transaction is paid or disputed.
- Send buyers a calendar invite the moment their booking is accepted.
- Only approve a listing once the seller has paid a listing fee on Stripe.
- Only approve a new user once they've paid a membership fee on Stripe.
- Add every new sign-up to your Mailchimp newsletter automatically.

[See all workflow templates →](/templates)

## What you'll need

- A Sharetribe marketplace on the **Build**, **Pro**, or **Extend** plan.
- An [n8n account](https://n8n.partnerlinks.io/n8n-nodes-sharetribe) - either self-hosted or n8n Cloud.
- About 10 minutes to follow the [setup guide](/getting-started).
